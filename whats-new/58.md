- **git-dash:** a new **Worktrees** tab lists every open worktree of the repositories of Git Dash and Repo Launcher, also worktrees outside your folders. Each row shows the changes, the commits that are not in the default branch, merged, the age of the last commit and a missing folder. You can open the worktree, remove it, delete its branch (local and remote), merge it into the default branch or open a pull request.
