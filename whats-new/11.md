- **agent-cards:** a new mod that shows the agents of the `main-dock` sidebar as cards. A small robot on each card acts out the status: it types on a laptop while the agent works, sleeps while it is idle, waves with a `!` when it needs you, and jumps when it is done. Each card shows the space, the tab, the time since the last change and a status pill. Click a card to go to the agent.
- **agent-cards:** put the pointer on a card for the pin button. Sub-agents show under their card; click one to peek at it, or fold the list.
- **agent-cards:** to go back to Herdr's own Agents list, turn off **Agent cards** in Herdr Mods → Settings. Turn it on again to get the cards back. `herdr-app do agent-cards.demo` shows the four robots at once.
- **main-dock:** the `agents` block of the sidebar now shows the agent cards. The tab bar makes the change by itself, keeps the size of the block, and follows the setting.
