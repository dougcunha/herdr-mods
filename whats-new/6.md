- **mods-hub:** a new **Settings** tab shows the settings of the kit mods in one place: Kit, agent-resume, changes-split, git-dash, kanban, Repo Launcher and Dock tabs. A change is saved at once and shows at once in the mod.
- **mods-hub:** the **Kit** section sets 1 language for the Kanban, Dock and Git Dash bars, or lets each layout decide as before. It also changes the shortcut of Herdr Mods.
- **kanban:** the gear button opens a menu with **Herdr settings** and **Herdr Mods**, when the mods-hub is installed. `kanban.gear` does the same from a key.
- **tabs-dock:** a new gear button, next to the density button, with the same menu. `tabs-dock.gear` does the same from a key.
- **git-dash:** Fetch on refresh that you choose (in the panel or in the mods-hub) now wins over the `fetch` prop, and a change shows at once.
- **kanban, tabs-dock, git-dash:** the kit language of the mods-hub changes the bar at once. The text in a search box changes after a restart.
