- **tokenhound:** a new mod that shows the quotas of the [TokenHound](https://github.com/dougcunha/TokenHound) HUD (Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, Cursor, Antigravity, OpenCode and Cline) in the Herdr app. The status bar shows one agent icon and one percent for each provider, with a green dot on the agent that works. The **Quota** button of the Dock and Kanban bars opens a card for each provider, with every quota, bar and reset countdown. A section under the agents list of `main-dock` is optional. It needs Windows and TokenHound 0.1.16 or newer, and it only reads TokenHound's `hud.json` file.
- **Setup:** the prompt installs `tokenhound` when TokenHound is on the machine, adds its status bar item to `main-dock` and `kanban`, adds the `quota` entry to `dock/panels.json`, and asks before it adds the sidebar section.
