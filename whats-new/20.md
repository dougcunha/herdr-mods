- **tab-namer:** new mod. F2 opens a dialog that renames the tab of the focused agent. The **Generate with AI** button reads the agent's session and asks a small model (Claude Haiku, or Codex `gpt-6-luna`) for a name of 2 to 5 words. You choose the program, the model and the language of the names in the Settings tab. The AI button needs Agent Resume.
- **kanban:** a card shows the tab name as its title when you named the tab. The agent's own title is in the tooltip. A pencil button on each card opens the Tab Namer dialog.
- **agent-cards:** a pencil button shows on a card under the pointer and opens the Tab Namer dialog.
- **agent-resume:** after `/clear` (Claude Code) or `/new` (Codex), it clears the tab name that Tab Namer saved for the old session, so the card shows the agent's own title again. The setup now also puts its block in the status bar of the Kanban layout: before, it did not run while the Kanban layout was shown.
