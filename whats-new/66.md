- **issues:** the issue page has a refresh button in its header (key `R`). It loads the issue again: its fields, comments and pull requests.
- **issues:** you can mention a person in a Jira comment. Type `@` and the start of a name in the comment box, then pick the person in the list. Jira tells the person about the comment.
- **issues:** **Reply** on a Jira comment adds your comment to the thread of that comment. The issue page shows the replies of a thread under its first comment.
- **issues:** **Assign to me** in the Details card of a Jira issue makes you the assignee.
- **issues:** click the story points in the Details card to change them. Enter saves, Esc cancels, and an empty field clears the estimate.
