- **agent-cards:** a new **Needs you first** switch. Click the bell in the header of the agents list: the agents that need you go to the top, and the other cards go to the bottom of the panel, under the line "Other agents". The other cards do not move when an agent asks for you. Click the bell again to turn it off.
- **agent-cards:** the same switch is in Herdr Mods → **Settings** → **Needs you first**. It is off by default. `herdr-app do agent-cards.needs-you` flips it.
