- **issues:** New **Repo** chip next to **Sprint** and **Next sprint** on the Jira list. It starts off, and the choice stays for every space and after a restart (also a switch in Settings). Turn it on to see only the open issues whose Jira component belongs to the repository of the shown space. It works together with the other filters and the sprint chips.
- **issues:** New **Repository components…** dialog (cog menu of the Issues panel, or PowerMods → Settings → Issues). It lists the components of the Jira project and saves your pick as a `components` line in `.herdr/jira.toml`, so the whole team can share it. Without that line, the Repo chip uses the folder name of the repository as the component name.
