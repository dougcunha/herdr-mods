- **quick-actions:** The pinned actions are items of the **Actions** menu of the bar, not buttons of their own. The menu shows **Actions** (it opens the list), a line, then the pinned actions. With no pinned action, **Actions** opens the list at once.
- **Dock, kanban:** A menu of a group button can have sections with a line between them (the new field `section` of `panels.json`). A group with only 1 command is a plain button.
