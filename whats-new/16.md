- **tokenhound:** the sidebar section has an arrow button in its head. It minimizes the section to one line with the icon and the session percent of each provider. Click it again to get the old height back. The same switch is in Herdr Mods → **Settings** → **Minimize the sidebar section**, and `herdr-app do tokenhound.minimize` flips it.
- **tokenhound:** each provider has an eye button to hide it: on the line of the sidebar section (put the pointer on it) and on its card in the Quota panel. The panel lists the hidden providers at the bottom; click one to show it again. The section head shows "N hidden"; click it to show all of them. The **Hidden providers** setting holds the same list.
