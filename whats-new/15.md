- **space-deck:** a new mod that replaces the Spaces list of the sidebar. It shows your spaces as cards or as a list; the button in the panel header changes the view. A card shows the folder, the git branch with ahead, behind and changed files, the tabs and terminals, the last activity, the agents with their status, and a note.
- **space-deck:** right-click a space to choose an icon (52 icons or an emoji) and a color, to pin it, or to write a note. The icon, the color, the note and the pin come back when you open the same repository again.
- **space-deck:** pinned spaces stay in the **Pinned** section at the top. Drag a space into the section, inside it to change the order, or out of it to unpin it.
- **space-deck:** put the pointer on a space to start a new agent in it or to close it. The + button opens Repo Launcher. A space with an agent that needs you gets a colored border; **Needs you first** in Herdr Mods → Settings puts these spaces at the top.
- **space-deck:** the **Recent** section at the bottom opens a closed space again with one click. The setup asks in which layouts the mod replaces Herdr's Spaces list.
