- **git-client:** Selecting an image (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, SVG, ICO, TIFF) in the Changes tab now previews the image directly in the diff pane and in the Expand dialog, showing Before/After (or Ours/Theirs for conflicts) in Unified or Split view, with dimensions and file size. SVG files also include an Image/Diff toggle to inspect or stage their text diff.
