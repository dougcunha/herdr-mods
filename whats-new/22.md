- **agent-resume:** on Windows, the Claude Code, Antigravity and OpenCode hooks now find `hc` also when its folder is not on the PATH (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\herdr-core`). Before, they failed without a word and no terminal got its session id: the resume after a reboot could not open the right session, and the **Generate with AI** button of Tab Namer did not show. Start a new agent session (or run `/clear` or `/resume`) to record the session of an agent that is already open.
