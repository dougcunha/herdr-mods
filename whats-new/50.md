- **repo-launcher:** a **No repository** section at the top of the list opens a terminal or an agent outside a repository, like Herdr's own New menu: **Terminal** adds a tab to the shown space, and **New space** makes a new space. `Ctrl+Enter` opens a shell at once; `Enter` shows the choices.
- **repo-launcher:** the choices have a **Space** row: open the terminal or the agent in a new space, or in any open space of the machine, not only in the space of the repository. Click a space, or press `Tab` and `Shift+Tab`. Without a choice, the launcher uses the space that **Reuse open space** picks.
