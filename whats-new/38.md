- **git-client:** a new **Branches** tab: your local branches (with their upstream, ↑ and ↓), the remote branches, the tags, the remotes and the worktrees, with a filter. A click on a row opens its menu: checkout, create a branch from it, rename, delete, merge, rebase, push, force push, set upstream, and for a remote fetch, rename, change URL and remove. **+** makes a branch, a tag, a remote or a worktree.
- **git-client:** worktrees: make one for a new or an existing branch in a folder next to the repository, open it in a Herdr space, lock it, remove it, and prune the ones whose folder is gone. Each one shows its number of changed files.
- **git-client:** a new **Stash** tab: your stashes with their files and diffs, **Apply**, **Pop**, **Drop** and **New stash**. The tab shows how many stashes there are.
- **git-client:** right-click **Push** to force push with `--force-with-lease`. Two new settings in Herdr Mods: **Pull** (Git config, fast-forward only, rebase or merge) and **Fetch prunes** (on by default).
- **git-client:** the right-click menus open at the pointer also in the panel dialog of the dock.
