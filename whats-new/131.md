- **quick-actions:** Pin an action to the bar of the Dock and Kanban layouts: click the pin of its row, or turn on **Bar button** in the form. The button has the name and the icon of the action. An action with **Only in** shows its button only while the focused terminal is in one of its folders or repositories.
- **quick-actions:** An **Actions** button in the bar opens the list.
- **Dock and Kanban:** The bar leaves out a button that a mod hides for now, for example a Quick Actions button whose filter does not match the focused terminal.
