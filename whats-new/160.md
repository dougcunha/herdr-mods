- **git-client:** Separated submodule branch-tip status (`latest`, `↑N`, `↓N`, `gone`, or `local` with a green/red indicator dot) from parent-repository pointer sync (`pointer`), and added submodule fetching (via **Fetch** in the Submodules header/tab, **Fetch remote** in a submodule's menu, and automatically when fetching the parent repository) so you can tell at a glance whether a submodule is on the latest commit of its branch.
