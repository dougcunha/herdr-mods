- **mods-hub:** the Mods tab has an on/off switch for each kit mod. A mod that is off shows no buttons in the bars, no blocks in the `main-dock` and `kanban` layouts and no status bar item, and it does no work. Nothing is deleted: turn it on again and everything comes back. `repo-launcher-status` goes with `repo-launcher`, and `files-md-menu` with `files-md`. The bars, the Kanban mod and Herdr Mods itself are always on.
- **mods-hub:** new commands for keys: `mods-hub.off-<mod>` and `mods-hub.on-<mod>` turn a mod off and on, and `mods-hub.open-<tab>` opens the dialog on a tab (`mods`, `settings`, `news`, `update`).
- **main-dock / kanban:** when `space-deck`, `files-md` or `changes-split` is off, the layout shows Herdr's own Spaces, Files or Changes panel in its place.
- **agent-cards:** its switch in the Mods tab is the same as its **Agent cards** setting. The pencil button goes away when `tab-namer` is off.
- **kanban:** the pencil button of the cards goes away when `tab-namer` is off.
- **space-deck:** the + button opens the New space menu when `repo-launcher` is off.
- **files-md:** the menu item **Open in Image Viewer** goes away when `image-viewer` is off.
