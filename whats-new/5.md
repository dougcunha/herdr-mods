- **mods-hub:** `mods-update` is now **Herdr Mods** (`mods-hub`). The setup moves it for you: it changes the status bar block in the `main-dock` and `kanban` layouts and removes the old folder. Your skipped version and your last check stay.
- **mods-hub:** a new **Mods** tab lists the mods in `~/.config/herdr-app/mods/` by kind: Kit, Third-party, Manual and Herdr. A row shows the description of a kit mod, its errors, and the files that you changed since the last setup run. **README of …** shows the README of the mod in the dialog.
- **mods-hub:** the dialog opens on **What's new** once for each new version, and after that on the last tab that you used.
- **mods-hub:** `ctrl-shift-h` (`cmd-shift-h` on macOS) opens the dialog, when the key is free.
- **All kit mods:** each mod has a `kit.json` with its description, which the **Mods** tab shows.
