- **dock:** the gear menu of the bar has a new item, **Bar buttons**. It lists the buttons of the panel group: move a button with ↑ and ↓, hide it or show it with the eye, and go back to the default with **Reset**. A hidden button still opens its panel with its key.
- **kanban:** the same **Bar buttons** item in the gear menu. The Kanban bar and the Dock bar share the order and the hidden buttons, so the right end of the bar stays the same when you switch layout.
- **dock, kanban:** the gear button opens its menu also without the `mods-hub` mod. Then the menu has **Herdr settings** and **Bar buttons**.
