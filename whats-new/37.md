- **git-client:** a new **Git** panel takes the place of the Changes and Graph panels. It has 2 tabs: **Changes** and **History**. The update moves your layouts, keys and settings to it, and removes `changes-split` and the third-party `git-graph`.
- **git-client:** in **Changes**, stage, unstage and discard each file, or all of them. A click on a hunk of the diff stages, unstages or discards only that hunk. The diff keeps the Unified / Split switch, and **Expand** opens it in a wide dialog.
- **git-client:** the commit box has a message of more lines, **Amend**, **Commit all** and **Push**. The **AI** button writes the message from the staged changes with Claude Code or Codex. Set the program, the model and the format in the Settings tab of Herdr Mods.
- **git-client:** during a merge, a rebase, a cherry-pick or a revert, a bar shows the files in conflict, with **Continue** and **Abort**.
- **git-client:** **History** is the commit graph of Git Graph for Herdr, with all its actions. A search field finds commits by text, hash, `author:` or `path:` in the whole history.
- **git-client:** the bar under the tabs shows the branch, ↑ and ↓, and the **Fetch**, **Pull** and **Push** buttons.
