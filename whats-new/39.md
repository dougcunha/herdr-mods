- **git-client:** during a merge, a rebase, a cherry-pick or a revert with conflicts, **Resolve with agent** opens a dialog: pick an installed agent, edit the prompt (it names the branches and the files in conflict), and the agent starts in a new tab of the space. It does not continue, commit or push: you check its work and click **Continue**.
- **git-client:** **File history** and **Blame** in the right-click menu of a file. History lists the commits that changed the file, also under an older name, with the diff of each one. Blame shows the commit, the author and the age of each line; click a line to see its commit.
- **git-client:** a new **Submodules** tab: each submodule with its state (ok, modified, out of sync, not initialized, conflict), **Update all**, **Sync** and **Add…**. A submodule's menu updates it to the recorded commit or from its remote branch, syncs its URL, removes it, or opens it in the client (with **Back**) or in a space.
